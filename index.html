Questions, answered

Which exam board and specification does Chainwise cover?

AQA A-Level Business, the new specification (7138) for first teaching from September 2026. Chainwise is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by AQA.

How accurate is the AI feedback?

It estimates a level and mark using published-style criteria and highlights how to improve. It is a practice aid, not an official mark. Always check with your teacher.

Do I need an account?

Not during the preview. Your name and progress are saved only in your browser on this device.
